3 · Fire season timing

When each year's burning started, peaked and ended in this region, so crews can plan for the critical period.

Start, midpoint and end are the dates by which 10%, 50% and 90% of the calendar year's harmonised activity had happened. Southern regions whose season spans December–February are better read from the calendar.

    8 · Method and validation

    –

    1. Clean. Keep vegetation fires (type 0): MODIS confidence ≥ 30, VIIRS confidence nominal or high.
    2. Grid. Count detections per 0.5° square per day, separately for each sensor. A detection is a fire pixel, not a whole fire.
    3. Calibrate. Learn how many MODIS detections match one VIIRS detection, per square, pulled toward the national factor where a square has little data.
    4. Validate. Learn on 2023–2024, test on 2025, a year the model never saw.
    K = ΣMODIS ÷ ΣVIIRS
    kcell = (Mcell + λ·K) ÷ (Vcell + λ), λ = 200
    Harmonised = VIIRS × kcell
